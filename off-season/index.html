No. It’s fully remote through the Inform app, so you can do it from anywhere in Australia. Your sessions, your running, your progress and your messages with Teneka all live in the app.

That’s exactly who it’s built for. Teneka reviews your injury history before writing anything, so your program targets the areas that have let you down before instead of ignoring them.
